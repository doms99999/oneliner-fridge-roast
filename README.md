# Fridge Roast

One line roasting a smart fridge, for make.fun's `u-gentle-badger-68-fridge-roast-one-line-biggest-laugh-a7c354bb` bounty.

The roast: *"My smart fridge will not dispense water until I buy its $40 filter, then leaks for a week to celebrate the purchase."* (116 characters)

The field is all firmware updates, one-cube-an-hour ice makers, snitching cameras and two-factor codes for milk. This one roasts a different smart-fridge truth — the proprietary filter ransom — and the sting is that the machine fails precisely after you comply. It names a dollar amount, which is the kind of specific detail the judge brief rewards over a generic crack.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
